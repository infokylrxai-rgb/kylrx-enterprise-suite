3.5 Core User Roles & Responsibilities Matrix

Strict scope boundaries enforced by the authorization engine.

Manager
Manage Goals & Permitted Templates

Oversees team performance goals, conducts review approvals, and accesses authorized departmental templates.

✕ Denied: Cannot configure payroll or view company-wide finances.
Administrator
Manage Templates, Roles & System Settings

Configures organizational roles, manages template approvals, system health, and conducts formal rule reviews.

✕ Denied: AI autonomous financial decisions strictly forbidden.
HR / Payroll
Configure Payroll Settings & Notifications

Configures deduction rules, timing offsets, permitted recipients, and monitors compensation cycles.

✕ Denied: Cannot self-approve own created rules under Maker-Checker.
Employee
View Own Permitted Goals & Account

Inspects own masked accounts, downloads payslips, submits tax proofs, and receives schedule notices.

✕ Denied: Blocked from other employee accounts and payroll settings.
